Two processor families built on the same Intel 18A process. One is tuned for heavy creation, gaming and local AI. The other brings the same modern design to everyday laptops at a friendlier price. Neither is the lesser choice.

01 · What they have in common

Same silicon DNA

Both families share the same foundation. The difference is how much of it each chip carries.

  • Intel 18A processRibbonFET transistors and PowerVia backside power delivery
  • Cougar Cove P-coresThe same performance-core design in both families
  • Darkmont LP E-coresLow-power efficiency cores for light background work
  • Xe3 graphicsThe newest Intel integrated GPU architecture
  • NPU 5A dedicated AI engine for on-device features
