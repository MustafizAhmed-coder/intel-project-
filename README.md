The Right Kind of Power — Intel Processor Choice Explorer
A visual, plain-language guide to choosing between Intel Core Ultra Series 3 (Panther Lake) and Intel Core Series 3 (Wildcat Lake) based on what a person actually does on a laptop.

Built around the idea: Different demands. Shared foundation. Your choice, your power.
What it does
The project turns a technical product distinction into an easy-to-follow story about choice. It presents two types of laptop user—people with demanding creation, gaming, productivity, and AI workloads, and people focused on everyday computing—and explains how each processor family is positioned for different needs.

The comparison covers:
- Intel Core Ultra Series 3 (Panther Lake): positioned for more demanding creation, gaming, productivity, and AI workloads.
- Intel Core Series 3 (Wildcat Lake): positioned for modern everyday computing and right-sized AI capabilities in more price-sensitive laptops.
- What they share: Intel 18A manufacturing technology, as described in the supplied challenge brief.
- Choosing a fit: a simple explanation of which family may be more suitable for different user priorities, without portraying either option as universally better.
This is an educational comparison experience, not a benchmark tool or a substitute for checking the specifications of an individual laptop or processor SKU.
The idea behind the design
Two kinds of busy. One shared foundation.

The visual concept contrasts a creator/gaming workspace with a study/everyday-work setup. The different environments make the use cases immediately recognisable, while the shared Intel 18A message connects the two sides. The poster-inspired visual language uses concise copy, strong type, and clear comparison panels rather than a dense technical specification sheet.
